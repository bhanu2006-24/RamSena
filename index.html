ABOUT RAM SENA (राम सेना)

✦ Sacred Philosophy: Based on the sacred Hindu history of The Ramayana. You are a humble generic Vanar or Bear devotee. You do NOT play as or alter the stories of Shri Ram, Lakshman, or Hanuman.

✦ Pure Service (Bhakti): Lift stones for Nal & Neel to build Ram Setu, gather wild fruits and flowers, and offer them with folded hands.

✦ Divine Intervention: In battle, there is NO Game Over. If your HP drops to 0, an arrow from Shri Ram strikes the foe and preserves your life.

✦ Controls: Move with WASD / Arrow Keys • Press [E] to Interact / Talk • Press [ENTER] / [ESC] to open Bag & Menu • Press [H] to Hail "जय श्री राम!".
