ABOUT RAM SENA (राम सेना)

✦ Sacred Philosophy: Based on the sacred history of The Ramayana. You are a humble generic Vanar or Bear devotee in the army of Shri Ram. Divine figures (Shri Ram, Lakshman, Hanuman) are revered and never altered.

✦ Pure Service (Bhakti): Lift stones for Nal & Neel to construct Ram Setu, gather wild fruits and flowers, and weave sacred garlands to offer with folded hands.

✦ Divine Intervention: In battle, there is NO Game Over. If your HP drops to 0, an arrow from Shri Ram strikes the foe and preserves your life.

✦ Controls: Move: Arrows / WASD • Interact: Z / E • Bag: B / I • Menu: M / Enter • Options: O • Volume: + / - • [H]: Hail "जय श्री राम!".
