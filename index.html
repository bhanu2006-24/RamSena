RAM SENA

राम सेना • The Sacred Vanguard

A minimalist top-down devotional RPG. Walk as a humble Vanar or Bear devotee in the army of Maryada Purushottam Shri Ram. Offer fruits, gather Setu stones, and march with the faithful.

ॐ

ABOUT RAM SENA

The Sacred Philosophy of Bhakti (Devotion)

✦ Sacred Context: This game is inspired by the timeless sacred epic, the Ramayana. The player is a humble, generic soldier—either a nimble Vanar from Kishkindha or a steadfast Bear of Jambavan’s clan.

✦ Immutable Rule: The player is NOT a divine figure. Shri Ram, Lakshman, and Hanuman are revered avatars and ideals of Dharma. The game never animates grand divine events or alters their sacred narratives; all epics are experienced through pure dialogue and humble service.

✦ Divine Intervention: In battle, the player never faces a game over. If strength falters, an arrow from Shri Ram strikes the foe, protecting the devotee with eternal grace.

✦ Controls: Move with WASD / Arrow Keys • Press [E] to Interact / Talk / Gather • Press [H] to Hail "जय श्री राम!" • Press [I] for Bag & Stats • Press [M] for Map Travel.

THE PROLOGUE

From Kishkindha to the Southern Shores

Following the friendship forged between Shri Ram and King Sugreev, millions of Vanars and Bears answered the call of Dharma.

Marching across forests, rivers, and rugged mountains, the great vanguard has arrived at the southern ocean at Rameshwaram. Ahead lies the roaring ocean, and across the waves lies Lanka, where Mother Sita remains steadfast in faith.

You step into the camp not for fame, but with folded hands, ready to lift every stone and offer every blossom in pure devotion.
