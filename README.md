# 🕉️ Ram Sena

**A devotional web game about service, faith, and the epic journey to Lanka.** 🙏

Ram Sena is a peaceful, minimalist web game designed as an act of devotion (Bhakti). Instead of playing as a grand hero or a divine figure, you experience one of history's greatest epics through the eyes of an ordinary, humble member of Shri Ram's army—a Vanar 🐒 (monkey) or a Bear 🐻. Your goal is not to change the story, but simply to serve, assist, and be present during the monumental events of the Ramayana.

---

## 📜 The Epic Story

The game is set against the backdrop of the grand history of the Ramayana:

The journey begins in the forests of Dandakaranya, where the demon king Ravan deceitfully abducts Mata Sita. In their agonizing search for her, Shri Ram and his brother Lakshman reach the monkey kingdom of Kishkindha. There, they befriend Sugreev and the great devotee Hanuman.

A massive search party is formed. Hanuman ji leaps across the vast ocean 🌊, discovers Mata Sita captive in the golden city of Lanka, and returns with the news. The entire Sena then marches to the southern shore of India, facing an endless ocean standing between them and Lanka.

Through the engineering of Nal and Neel, and the absolute devotion of the army writing Shri Ram's name on heavy stones, a miraculous bridge (Ram Setu 🌉) is built across the sea. The army crosses into Lanka, fighting a fierce war ⚔️ against Ravan's demon forces. In the end, truth triumphs over ego: Ravan is defeated, Mata Sita is rescued, and the divine couple is reunited. 🪷

**📍 Where this game takes place:**
The game begins exactly at the shores of the ocean, just before the bridge is built, and concludes during the great war in Lanka.

---

## 🪷 The Philosophy of the Game

This game is built on a deep respect for the sacred history it depicts.

- **You are not the Idol:** You cannot play as Shri Ram, Lakshman, or Hanuman. Their divine actions are beyond human control and are described respectfully through text stories within the game. You are a humble servant. 🧎‍♂️
- **The Power of Service:** Your role is to perform simple daily tasks—gathering stones 🪨, offering fruits 🍎, and protecting the camp.
- **Divine Protection 🛡️:** In this game, you cannot die. If you are ever defeated in battle, an arrow 🏹 from Shri Ram instantly strikes your enemy, saving you. _When Shri Ram is on your side, you never lose._

## 🎮 How to Play

The game is divided into two distinct phases, focusing on simple daily routines, gathering, and devotion.

### 🌉 Phase 1: Building the Ram Setu (The Bridge)

The Sena is camped at the beach. You must help gather materials to cross the ocean.

- **⛺ The Camp:** Meet static members of the army, including Shri Ram, Lakshman, Hanuman, Sugreev, and Jambavan. You can speak with them or offer them items.
- **🌳 The Forest:** Walk among the trees and mountains. Shake trees to gather apples 🍎, oranges 🍊, and flowers 🌸. Search the ground for sacred stones 🪨.
- **🏖️ The Beach:** Gather coconuts 🥥 from the trees. Bring your gathered stones to Nal and Neel so they can construct the bridge.
- **📿 Crafting:** If you collect enough flowers, you can weave a beautiful garland to offer at the camp.

### ⚔️ Phase 2: The War in Lanka

Once the bridge is complete, the story moves across the ocean.

- **⛺ Lanka Camp & Forest:** Similar to the first phase, but with new conversations reflecting the ongoing war.
- **🏹 The Battlefield:** Venture into the field to face Ravan’s forces. Engage in simple combat using basic moves like scratch, bite, or throwing wood.
- **📜 Updates from the War:** Major events (like Hanuman ji bringing the Sanjeevani mountain 🏔️) happen in the background and are shared with you through text updates when you return to camp.

## 🌐 Getting Started

This game is designed to be accessible to everyone. There are no complex installations or high-end graphics required.

1. 💻 Simply open the game link in any modern web browser on your phone or computer.
2. ⌨️ Use the **Arrow Keys** (⬆️ ⬇️ ⬅️ ➡️) or **W, A, S, D** on your keyboard to move your character.
3. 🖐️ Use the **Spacebar** or **Enter** key to interact with trees, gather items, and talk to comrades.
4. 📖 Read the text box at the bottom of the screen to follow the story, manage your inventory, and see the results of your actions.

**Welcome to the Sena. Serve with a pure heart, and enjoy the journey!** 🙏 🕉️
